// What LogicSmith Is

One companion.
Your entire journey.

📖

Dev Diary

Your game lives here. Every phase, every task, every AI instruction — documented in your Dev Diary. Come back to any session, pick up exactly where you left off, and build a record of everything you made and why.

⬡

Build Your Game

Your GDD becomes a full Implementation Plan — phases, tasks, and Blueprint instructions tailored to your specific game. The Node Canvas visualizes your logic before you wire a single node. You always know what to build next.

🔧

Blueprint Utility

Already know what you're building? Use the Blueprint Utility standalone — no project required. Get AI instructions for any Blueprint asset, any time, saved to your personal repository.

// The LogicSmith Journey

From idea.
To your game.

01

Shape Your Idea

The Game Design Planner asks the right questions and turns your answer into something concrete — before AI ever gets involved.

02

Define Your Game

Take your planner export to Claude or ChatGPT. Get a full Game Design Document built around your vision — not a generic one.

03

Build Your Plan

Your GDD becomes a phase-by-phase Implementation Plan for your specific game. You always know what to build next.

04

Work Phase by Phase

Your Dev Diary organises your build into phases and tasks. Start a task, get AI instructions, document your progress, mark it complete.

05

Wire Your Blueprints

Structured AI instructions, a Python scaffolder, and a visual Node Canvas for every Blueprint. You understand what you're building — and you build it yourself.

06

Your Game

Built by you. Every decision made by you. Every Blueprint wired by you. The knowledge is yours to keep.
